## Positioning decision

Primary identity:

> Industrial Engineer who combines production planning and process improvement with hands-on digital systems development.

This is stronger and more credible than presenting two unrelated careers. The industrial-engineering foundation stays primary; React, TypeScript, Supabase, Three.js, and Vercel become evidence that Abdelaziz can digitize workflows, specifications, and operational decisions.

## What was inconsistent

- The English Europass resume used three pages, with a largely empty final page and repeated education/skills wording.
- The Arabic resume was visually stronger but did not show the technical portfolio or current programming stack.
- The public LinkedIn profile positioned Abdelaziz mainly as an Industrial Engineering graduate and production-planning candidate; it did not surface systems development.
- The public About preview mentioned Lean, supply chain, and process improvement but omitted the bilingual web systems, Supabase security work, and 3D product configuration.
- Important experience from Delta CJSC and Murat Şahin Orman Ürünleri was not clearly visible in the public LinkedIn view.
- The resumes included national ID, birth date, nationality, and work-permit details. These were removed from the public portfolio and updated CVs because they are not needed for initial recruitment screening.
- The internship bullets described duties but rarely showed the operating context, action, and practical result together.
